Which plants should we add next?

The Wausau Grower is Wausau Pilot & Review's free planting calendar and growing guide for Marathon County. It covers the 31 plants below, and we plan to add more, especially flowers. Please write in the plants you would most like to see. Naming the plant is all we need: we compile the planting dates and growing details from published Extension sources and check every date against at least two of them.
