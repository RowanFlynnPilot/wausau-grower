What these mean

Zone 4b / 5a: Wausau sits on the line between the two on the 2023 USDA map. By ZIP code, Wausau and Schofield–Weston are 4b; Rothschild and Mosinee are 5a.

~May 15 and ~Oct 1: the frost dates the tool plans around. NOAA's medians for the Wausau airport are May 6 and Oct 4, but in one year out of ten, frost comes after May 21 in spring or by Sep 21 in fall, so the tool leans toward the safe side. The calendar shades that risk, fading as the odds fall.

~139 days is the span between those two dates. NOAA's median frost-free season is 150 days.
